Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Gigwise is a freelance marketplace that ranks freelancers and projects with an explained match score. This page explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to have it deleted. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address. If you sign up with a password, it is handled by our login provider (Supabase) and stored only in scrambled form; Gigwise never sees it. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us; we only use your email address to identify your account.
  • Your profiles: what you enter. For clients: your name or company name. For freelancers: name, title, skills, hourly rate, years of experience, hours per week, location, bio, portfolio, work history, education, certifications and languages.
  • What you do on Gigwise: projects you post, proposals, offers, contracts, milestones, reviews, messages, notifications and saved items.
  • On your device: your login session and preferences (such as night or day mode) are kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider records basic request information, such as IP address and time, to run and protect the service.

How we use it

  • To run the marketplace: show your profile and projects, deliver proposals, offers and messages, and send you notifications.
  • To calculate match scores. Scores use only skills, how well the project text and profile overlap, budget fit, experience and ratings. We never use your name, gender, location, photo or any other personal attribute to score you.
  • To keep Gigwise safe: to stop abuse and make sure people can only see and change their own private data.

Who can see what

  • Other signed-in users can see freelancer profiles, client names, open projects and reviews.
  • Only the two people involved can see a proposal, offer, contract, milestone or message thread between a client and a freelancer.
  • Only you can see your notifications and saved items.
  • The demo ("Try the demo") contains sample people and projects only. It is kept completely separate from real accounts.

Services we rely on

We share data only with the providers that run Gigwise for us, and only as needed:

  • Supabase stores the database and handles login (servers in Seoul, South Korea).
  • Render hosts the website and server (servers in Singapore).
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • jsDelivr and Google Fonts deliver code libraries and fonts to your browser, so they see your IP address when the page loads.
  • Anthropic (Claude), only if we turn on AI-written match explanations (currently off). We would then send project details and profile facts such as skills and experience, never names or locations, to write the explanation.

Because these providers are in other countries, your data may be stored and processed outside the country where you live.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data while your account is open. To delete your account and everything linked to it, email us from the address you signed up with at aiteam27@byop.mesaschool.co. We will delete it within 30 days. Reviews you left may be kept without your name so other people's ratings stay accurate.

Your choices

  • You can view and edit your profile at any time in the app.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, using the email above.

Age

Gigwise is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell signed-in users in the app.
